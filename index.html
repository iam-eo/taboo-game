Taboo Guide & Help

Quick Start

  1. Enter team names and player names.
  2. Tap START MATCH.
  3. Pass the phone to the first clue-giver.
  4. Clue-giver taps START TURN, then controls the 3 buttons.

Objective

Get your teammates to guess the Target Word before time runs out. You can say anything, EXCEPT the target word or any of the 5 Forbidden Words.

Roles

  • Clue-Giver: Holds the phone, sees the card, and taps the buttons.
  • Guessing Team: Shouts answers based on the clues.
  • Watchdogs (Opposing Team): Sits nearby, listens, and catches violations.

Teams & Players

For each team, select the number of players and click "Edit" to type their names. The app automatically rotates them as the clue-giver each round.

During the Turn

Hand the phone to the active Clue-Giver. They tap START TURN. The timer starts and the card reveals.

AI Builder

Tap the database icon at the top right to open decks. You can use the AI Builder to generate 50 fresh cards for any category using a free Gemini API key.
